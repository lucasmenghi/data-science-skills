# Example — Classification project

## Business
Increase retained margin compared with the current campaign.

## Analytical
Deliver meaningful lift in the first operational deciles.

## Technical
Complete batch scoring before the campaign cut-off.

## Operational
Limit the prioritized population to the contact capacity.

## Guardrails
Do not significantly increase complaints or contact cost.

## Decision
- GO: target and guardrails achieved.
- REVISE: minimum achieved, target not achieved.
- NO-GO: minimum or critical guardrail not achieved.
